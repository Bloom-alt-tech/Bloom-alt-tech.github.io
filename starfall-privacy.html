Starfall Bastion privacy policy

Starfall Bastion · Last updated 6 November 2026

Starfall Bastion ("the game") is a single-player game for Android made by Leb_Lord. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information the game stores

Your progress, such as levels cleared, coins, weapons, gear and settings, is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Android may include it in your device's own backup to your Google account if backup is turned on in your phone's settings.

You can erase all progress at any time with Settings > Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the game.

Information the game does not collect

The game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, camera, microphone or any account login. It has no chat and no way for players to contact each other.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game can remind you when your daily reward is ready or your colony storage is full. These reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; no information is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the game opens your phone's normal share menu with a message about your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional reward videos you choose to watch, and an occasional full-screen ad between levels (removed by the No Ads Pass). Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalized ads.

Google AdMob is a third-party service. It may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about how ads are viewed, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads).

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. To confirm purchases and restore them on a new device, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID (not your name or email).

RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and requests

We hold no personal data on our own systems. For data processed by Google AdMob or RevenueCat, use their privacy tools linked above, or contact us and we will help.
