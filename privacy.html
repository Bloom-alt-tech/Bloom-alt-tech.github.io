Privacy Policy

Bloomlands · Last updated 27 October 2026

Leb_Lord ("we") makes the mobile game Bloomlands (the "App"). This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App. We do not run our own servers and we never see your game data.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, scores, settings, coins, trophies and Bloom Town are stored only on your device. On Android this data may be included in your device's own Google backup. We do not receive it.

The spoken announcer ("Great!", "Excellent!") uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. The App does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking)
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • information about ad interactions, app usage and diagnostics

This is used to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states) the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases (Google Play, Apple, RevenueCat)

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases. RevenueCat receives the purchase details and an anonymous app user ID. We never receive your payment details.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we hold no personal data on our own systems, requests about advertising or purchase data are handled by Google and RevenueCat; we are happy to help you reach them. Deleting the App removes all game data stored on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available at this address, with the date at the top.
