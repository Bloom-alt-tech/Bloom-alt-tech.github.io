Gatefall privacy policy Gatefall and Gatefall 3D · Last updated 23 November 2026 Gatefall and Gatefall 3D ("the games") are single-player action games for Android made by Leb_Lord. This policy explains what information the games use. It covers both games. Information the games store Your progress, such as levels cleared, coins, gems, champions, weapons, skins, upgrades and settings, is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Android may include it in your device's own backup to your Google account if backup is turned on in your phone's settings. Uninstalling a game erases its progress from the device. Information the games do not collect The games do not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, camera, microphone or any account login. They have no chat and no way for players to contact each other. Advertising The games show ads through Google AdMob: optional reward videos you choose to watch (for example to revive or to earn extra coins or gems), and an occasional full-screen ad between rounds (removed by the Remove Ads purchase). Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the games ask for your consent through Google's consent form first, and you can change your choice at any time with Settings > Ad privacy. Google AdMob is a third-party service. It may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about how ads are viewed, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites Purchases In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The games use Unity In-App Purchasing to connect to Google Play Billing, confirm purchases and restore one-time purchases on a new device. Unity may receive technical information about the store connection, but not your name, email or payment details. Google Play's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy · Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy Children The games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Your choices and requests We hold no personal data on our own systems. For data processed by Google AdMob, Google Play or Unity, use their privacy tools linked above, or contact us and we will help.
